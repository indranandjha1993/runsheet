# Code of conduct

Be straightforward and be kind. Argue about the work, not the person doing it.

Concretely: assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith, say what you mean
without softening it into vagueness, and accept a correction without treating it as an attack.
Harassment, personal abuse, and unwelcome attention are not tolerated.

If someone is behaving this way, report it through the repository's private contact. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block accounts.
